General Information

What services do you offer?

We provide delivery services across all regions of Algeria, with a strong focus on speed and reliability.

Which areas do you cover?

We operate in all Algerian provinces without exception.

Do you offer shipping services for individuals or only for businesses?

Our services are available to everyone — individuals, online stores, and companies.

Do you have a mobile app?

Currently, we do not have a mobile app, but we are working on launching one soon to make tracking and service requests easier.

Shipping & Delivery

How long does delivery take?

Delivery usually takes between 1 to 3 days, depending on the destination.

Can I track my shipment?

Yes, we provide a tracking service. However, due to the short delivery distances, tracking may not always be very detailed.

Do you offer cash on delivery (COD)?

Yes, we use the cash-on-delivery system, as it is safer and more convenient for both us and the customer.

Can I change the delivery address after the shipment is sent?

Yes, but you'll need to visit one of our branches or contact us directly to explain the situation.

Is same-day delivery available?

It depends on the destination and the availability of shipments at that time. We always aim to deliver as quickly as possible.

Pricing & Payment

How much does shipping cost?

Shipping costs vary depending on the province. You can get an estimate when placing your order.

Do you offer discounts for regular shipments or online stores?

Not yet, but we plan to introduce special offers and packages for online sellers very soon.

What payment methods are available?

All payments are made in cash upon delivery.

Packaging & Restrictions

Do I have to package the shipment myself?

Yes. Once you print the shipping label, you should package your item securely and attach the label clearly and visibly.

What items are prohibited from shipping?

We do not accept any illegal items such as drugs, tobacco, or hazardous materials.

Can I ship perishable items like food?

Yes, but we are not responsible for any damage or spoilage during transit.

Issues & Complaints

What should I do if my shipment is delayed?

You can visit the nearest branch or contact us directly for an update and clarification.

What happens if my shipment gets lost or damaged?

We always do our best to prevent such incidents. However, if they occur, we take full responsibility and offer compensation based on the value of the shipment.
